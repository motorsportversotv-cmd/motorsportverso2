As novidades da Nascar Brasil 2026

A principal novidade a mudança de chassis para "Rise26" , pelo menos a aparência mais parecida com a Nascar Americana, mas com o defeito de ter motor V6 invez de V8. Equipes , se juntam a categoria a Pole Motorsport, a Cavaleiro Sports. The main change is the switch to the ‘Rise26’ chassis, which at least looks more like the American Nascar, but with the drawback of having a V6 engine instead of a V8. Teams joining the category include Pole Motorsport and Cavaleiro Sports.

The calendar, inspired by American NASCAR, will feature a Clash at the Autodromo Circuito Paramericano in the state of São Paulo, which will also be the first event with races at this racetrack, which until now had been used for training, testing and track days.
