Novidades Fia WEC 2026

Hyundai\Genesis: The South Korean brand enters with the Genesis sports brand in 2026 with the GMR 001, with a base chassis from Oreca, operated by the Idec Sport team, with drivers Pipo Derani, André Lotterer as highlights.

Porsche:

A marca Alemã estará fora do Fia WEC em 2026 na Hypercar com a equipe de fabrica , por causa de “motivos financeiros” e principalmente do BOP (Balence de performance) ser nada favorável a eles em 2025, principalmente em Le Mans, a possibilidade de retorno más vai precisar de investimento.

Porsche: The German brand will be out of the FIA WEC in 2026 in Hypercar with the factory team, due to “financial reasons” and mainly because the BOP (Balance of Performance) will not be favorable to them in 2025, especially at Le Mans. The possibility of a return will require investment.
